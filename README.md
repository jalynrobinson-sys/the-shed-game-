[THE SHED — Interactive Story Game (Super Duper Detailed) 

Working Title: Bailee and the Secret of the Talking Backyard — The Shed 

Format: Web-first (mobile-friendly) interactive story adventure (no fail states) 

Target Age: 6–10 (with optional “Read-to-Me” support) 

Session Length: 15–30 minutes (Chapter 1) 

Core Promise: A cozy, funny, magical backyard adventure where Bailee’s curiosity unlocks the world’s biggest secret: her pets can talk — and they’ve been waiting for her to notice. 

 

1) FULL GAME DESIGN DOCUMENT (GDD) 

1.1 Vision Statement 

A narrative-first cozy adventure that feels like a playable picture book. Players guide Bailee through one backyard map, meeting talking animals through small, safe interactions. The emotional arc centers on being seen (Bailee’s sensitivity to being overlooked), belonging, and curiosity as a superpower. 

1.2 Player Fantasy 

“I’m the kid who discovers the backyard’s secret.” 

“My pets are my team.” 

“I get to explore without being punished.” 

“My choices create funny reactions and warm connection.” 

1.3 Pillars (Non‑Negotiables) 

Cozy Safety: No death, threats, or scary consequences. Mystery stays playful. 

Curiosity‑Driven Progress: Progress comes from noticing, interacting, asking. 

Character Comedy: Dutchess chaos, Phoenix sighs, Chanel sass, Dragon mystery. 

Emotional Warmth: Bailee’s need to be noticed is validated, not shamed. 

Picture‑Book Clarity: Bright, readable composition; big buttons; simple UI. 

1.4 Core Loop 

Explore (tap/click to move; subtle shimmer on interactables) 

Interact (objects + animals + environment) 

Receive (dialogue, jokes, clues, “Wonder Sparks”) 

Unlock (new zone emphasis, animal trust, shed progress) 

Story Beat (micro cutscene / illustrated panel) 

Repeat until Shed Reveal 

1.5 Tone & Writing Rules 

Bailee: bold, playful, snack‑obsessed, soccer‑obsessed, wants attention. 

Animals: distinct “TV show” voices; argue/tease; never mean. 

No lectures. “Heart line” moments are 1–2 sentences max. 

Spanish immersion flavor: occasional natural words (“¡Mira!”, “¿Qué es eso?”). 

 

2) GAMEPLAY SYSTEMS 

2.1 Controls 

Move: tap/click ground → Bailee walks (bouncy skip). 

Interact: tap/click interactable when in range. 

Inspect Mode: long‑press (mobile) / right‑click (desktop) to zoom + Bailee thoughts. 

Accessibility: 

Read‑to‑Me narration (toggle) 

Text size S/M/L 

Captions for SFX/music 

Reduced motion 

Highlight interactables (stronger shimmer) 

2.2 Progression & Gating (Shed Unlock) 

Goal: Open the shed. 

How: Earn 3 Wonder Sparks (represented as 3 icon slots on the shed lock UI). These are earned by completing three character‑driven micro‑quests: 

Dutchess → Wonder Spark #1 (Pawprint) 

Phoenix → Wonder Spark #2 (Bone) 

Chanel → Wonder Spark #3 (Sparkle) 

When all 3 are filled, the shed lock turns from sleepy gray to warm gold and the Knock interaction becomes available. 

Important: No checklist. Progress is visual via the lock’s 3 slots. 

2.3 Interaction Types 

Pick Up (treat bag, brush, feather, shiny tag) 

Use (use treat on Dutchess; brush Phoenix; feather to engage Chanel) 

Listen (tap to “lean in” for audio cue) 

Knock (shed knock rhythm moment) 

Pet (consent‑based) (Chanel only after permission; Phoenix always; Dutchess wiggles) 

2.4 Inventory (Kid‑Friendly) 

Backpack icon opens a bottom tray with up to 6 large slots. 

No crafting chains. 

Items are used 1–2 times max. 

Tooltips are funny. 

Chapter items: 

Crunchy Treat 

Fruit Snacks 

Cheese Stick 

Phoenix Brush 

Fancy Feather 

Mystery Tag (Shiny) 

2.5 Hint System 

Idle 12 seconds → Phoenix hint bubble: “Maybe check the garage path.” 

Tap shed too early → Bailee: “Not yet… it’s like it’s waiting for something.” 

If player ignores Chanel → Chanel meow + feather shimmer intensifies. 

2.6 No Fail States 

Wrong choice = alternate funny line. 

No timer, no punishment. 

Progress always recoverable.

Trigger: 3 slots filled. 

Player action: Tap knock 3 times in any rhythm (perfect rhythm = extra sparkle). 

Result: Lock clicks open; cutscene.
